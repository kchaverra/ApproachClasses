    Set up the photos

    Add the answers for each photo. Students see the photo and complete four sentences: This is ___. He is from ___. He is a ___. He has ___ hair. Leave country or job empty to skip that sentence for one photo.

    Wrong answers for the word bank

    The word bank mixes these in with the correct answers, so students must really choose. Separate words with commas.

    Add other correct answers with commas. For example, country: USA, the USA, America. The first answer goes on the worksheet and in the word bank.
